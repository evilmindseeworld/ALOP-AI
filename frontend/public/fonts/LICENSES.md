# Font licences

All four families are self-hosted with permission of their licences. None may be
replaced or added to without checking the new font's licence permits self-hosting.

| Family | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Switzer | ITF Free Font Licence | https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer |
| Clash Grotesk | ITF Free Font Licence | https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/clash-grotesk |
| JetBrains Mono | SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |
| Instrument Serif | SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 | https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif |

The ITF Free Font Licence permits use, including commercially, and permits
self-hosting. It does not permit selling the fonts themselves.

The SIL OFL permits use, modification and redistribution, including bundled with
a web application, provided the fonts are not sold on their own.
